I'm Thera Jan. I was a scholar-monk. Today let me tell you a short story about this temple, Wat Chet Yot.

I once wrote something called a "guide to conduct" for King Tilokarat. And that same King Tilokarat is also the one who built this temple, Wat Chet Yot. Seeing that the man I once advised went on to leave behind such a grand temple gives me a strange kind of feeling.

Even after I passed away, this temple's story continued. In 1511 — to place it in time, that was right around when Michelangelo was about to finish the ceiling of the Sistine Chapel — a boundary-consecration ceremony was held at this temple. But among the roughly twenty monks listed as attending, not a single one was from Chiang Mai, or so it's said. Scholars later argued over that very list, debating who could have possibly been the author of a certain old record. To be honest, I don't know the answer either. I was simply someone who happened to know the king who built this temple.

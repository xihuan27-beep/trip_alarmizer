I'm Chiang Mai Gate. I stand at the southeast corner of the city wall. Today let me tell you a short story tied to my name.

I actually wasn't always called "Chiang Mai Gate." Long ago, I was known by the name "Pratu Tai Wiang." As time passed, the name people used for me changed too, and now everyone simply calls me Chiang Mai Gate. Even a single name like mine carries the marks of passing time.

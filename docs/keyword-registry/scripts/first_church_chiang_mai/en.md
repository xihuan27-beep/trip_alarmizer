I'm Daniel McGilvary. I was a missionary. Today let me tell you the story tied to this place, the site of Chiang Mai's first church.

This land was given to me by the Kawilorot family. Besides building a church here, I did some other things too. I was the one who first brought quinine and smallpox vaccination to this region. In an unfamiliar land, I suppose I was trying to bring both faith and medicine at once.

Even after I passed away, the look of this land kept changing. From 1917 to 1968, the riverbank was a gentle, natural slope. But after 1968, a vertical concrete embankment and a four-lane road were built right here, cutting off what had once been a natural path down to the river. It's become a rather different scene now from the one I first set foot on.

I'm Pratu Saen Pung. Today let me tell you a short story about another name of mine.

Besides "Pratu Saen Pung," I was also known by the name "Pratu Haiya." That name wasn't given to me for no reason. It's because passing through me led on to a cremation ground called Haiya. So my old name itself carried the meaning of a path people once walked down to send someone off on their final journey.

I'm Camadevi. I'm said to be the queen who founded and ruled Hariphunchai. Today let me tell you the story tied to this place, Chet Lin. There are actually several versions of the stories told about me, but here's the one told at this spot.

A Lawa king named Wiranka once proposed marriage to me. When I refused him, he grew angry and led his army in an attack. But in the end, he was defeated right here at Chet Lin, by my own sons. It's said to have happened sometime around the 8th century.

The story surrounding this same Wiranka is told a bit differently elsewhere. But at least here, at Chet Lin, this is how it's remembered.
